b. A specific place, for example: "Do you have any photos taken at a certain travel destination?"
c. An important person or item, for example: "Have you ever received a special gift or card?"
d. A touching or happy moment, for example: "Is there an experience that made you feel particularly joyful or touched?"
e. A turning point in life, for example: "Have you had an experience that changed the way you view life?"
f. A special event, for example: "Have you ever participated in a special party or event?"
h. Any clue related to the user's unique experience, for example: "Is there a photo that reminds you of a special scene?"
Each memory clue should contain details and encourage the user to share their specific memories. Note that this part should be limited to 20-30 words.
3. Then, from the user's perspective, provide three preset topics that can be expanded on, and each topic should end with "...".
